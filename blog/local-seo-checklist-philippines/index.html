The One-Weekend Local SEO Checklist for a Philippine Business

A weekend is enough time to handle the local SEO fundamentals that matter most: verifying your Google Business Profile, filling it out completely, fixing your directory listings, and setting up a real system for collecting reviews going forward. It is not enough time to out-rank an established competitor overnight, but it is enough to stop actively losing customers to a broken or incomplete online presence, which is the more urgent problem for most businesses starting from zero.

This pulls together the individual pieces from the rest of this guide into a single order of operations, so you can sit down on a Saturday and actually finish something, instead of reading ten articles and closing every tab still unsure where to start. It's the same foundation we build with local businesses every day.

Before you start: what you'll need

  • Access to your Google account, or a plan to create a business-owned one
  • Your actual business hours, services, and a few good photos, ready to upload
  • A phone, for both the video verification step and testing your own listings the way a customer would see them

Saturday morning: the profile itself

1. Check if a Google Business Profile already exists. Search your business name and city from your phone. Claim an existing listing if you find one, rather than creating a duplicate.

2. Create or claim your profile at business.google.com, using a business-owned Google account, not a personal one tied to a single employee. Our full walkthrough on setting up your Google Business Profile covers each field in detail.

3. Choose the most specific accurate category available. This single field does more for your visibility than almost anything else on the profile.

4. Complete every section: hours, a real description in plain language, your services list, and at least several genuine photos of your actual location and work, not stock images.

5. Start verification. In the Philippines, this most often means a short video walkthrough of your storefront, filmed live in one continuous take, clearly showing your signage. Submit this as early in the day as possible, since review can take a few business days, and you want that clock running while you finish everything else.

Saturday afternoon: reviews and your review system

6. Get your direct review link from the "Ask for reviews" option in your Google Business Profile dashboard, and generate a QR code version of it.

7. Save a review-request message as a template in Messenger, worded genuinely rather than generically, ready to send the moment a customer's service or order is complete. Our guide to getting more Google reviews has wording that works.

8. If you already have happy past customers, send a short, personal request to a handful of them today, rather than waiting for new customers to trickle in over the coming weeks.

9. Print or display your QR code somewhere customers naturally pause, your counter, a receipt, or near the exit.

Sunday morning: consistency across the web

10. Decide on one official version of your business name, address, and phone number, and make sure your website and Google profile match each other exactly.

11. Search your business name and phone number to find any old or orphaned directory listings you may not know exist, and note anything that needs correcting.

12. Claim and correct what you find, prioritizing higher-authority Philippine directories like Yelp Philippines and Yellow Pages PH first. Consistent citations are a core part of ranking in the local pack.

Sunday afternoon: keywords and a plan going forward

13. Spend twenty minutes on real keyword research: type your service plus your city into Google, note the autocomplete suggestions, check the "People also ask" box, and check related searches at the bottom of the page.

14. Work the language you found into your Google profile description and services list, and note it for future website copy.

15. Set a recurring monthly reminder to check your profile is still accurate, add a couple of new photos, and respond to any reviews you have not yet answered.

What you will not finish this weekend, and that's fine

Ranking above established competitors, building a large base of citations, and developing a steady, months-long flow of reviews all take sustained time, not a single weekend. This checklist is not meant to finish that work. It is meant to make sure your foundation is solid enough that the ongoing effort you put in afterward actually has something to build on.

The most common way this checklist gets skipped halfway

The most common failure is not laziness, it is stopping after the profile is set up and never building the reviews and citations system that keeps momentum going afterward. A profile finished on a Saturday and never touched again slowly loses the ground it gained. Set the reminder in step 15 and actually keep it.

What comes after this weekend

Once these fundamentals are in place, the ongoing work is mostly two things repeated consistently: keeping your review request system running after every transaction, and keeping your directory listings accurate as anything about your business changes. Neither is complicated. Both are easy to let slide without a system holding you to it. When you're ready to go further, our SEO & AI visibility service takes it from foundation to sustained ranking.
