How to Show Up When Customers Ask ChatGPT to Recommend a Business Like Yours

You show up when someone asks ChatGPT or a similar AI tool for a local business recommendation largely the same way you show up in a Google search: by having a real, well-established online presence that the AI can find and trust, meaning a solid website, an active Google Business Profile, genuine reviews, and mentions across the web. There is no separate registration or paid placement for this. It is earned the same way visibility has always been earned, just read by a different kind of system.

More customers are starting their search for a local business with a question typed into an AI chat instead of a Google search box. It is still a small share of overall search behavior, but it is growing, and it is worth understanding honestly rather than either ignoring it or overreacting to it. It's a natural extension of our SEO & AI visibility work.

How these tools actually find local recommendations

Different AI tools work differently, and the honest answer is that this landscape is still evolving quickly. What is broadly true across most of them:

  • They draw on real-time web search results when the question needs current or local information, in a similar way to how Google's own AI Overviews pull from ranked pages.
  • They favor businesses that already have visible, consistent information across the web, meaning a real website, an active Google Business Profile, and genuine third-party mentions, rather than relying on old training data alone.
  • They cannot recommend what they cannot find. A business with no meaningful online presence is functionally invisible to these tools, the same way it would be invisible to a normal Google search.

Why this is not a separate project from your existing SEO work

If you have already been working through the rest of this cluster, setting up your Google Business Profile properly, collecting real reviews, keeping your citations consistent, you are already doing the work that makes AI recommendation more likely, not a different set of tasks. The overlap is almost total. This is worth knowing because it means you do not need a second strategy running alongside your regular local SEO. You need the first one done well.

What's different about being recommended by an AI tool versus showing up in Google

A Google local pack result gives a customer three or more options to compare and choose between themselves. An AI recommendation often surfaces fewer choices, sometimes just one, phrased as a direct suggestion. That makes the businesses that do get mentioned carry more weight in that single moment, and it makes the businesses that do not get mentioned effectively invisible in that conversation, with no second or third listing for the customer to notice instead.

What you can reasonably do about it

Keep your fundamentals genuinely strong, not just checked off. A complete, accurate, actively maintained Google Business Profile and website give any AI system the clearest possible signal about who you are and what you offer.

Make sure your business is described consistently everywhere it appears. The same inconsistency that hurts your Google ranking, mismatched names, addresses, or descriptions across different sites, also makes it harder for an AI system to confidently identify your business as a real, single, trustworthy entity.

Earn genuine mentions, don't chase fake ones. Google has explicitly warned against seeking inauthentic mentions across the web as a tactic, and the same caution reasonably applies here. A real mention from a real local blog, group, or directory carries weight. A purchased or fabricated one does not, and it can damage trust if discovered.

Be patient with a new business. Just as with traditional search ranking, an AI tool is more likely to confidently recommend a business with an established, verifiable history than a brand-new listing with almost no track record behind it yet.

What to skip entirely

The same warning applies here as with Google's AI Overviews: there is no special file, no hidden markup, and no paid trick that gets a business recommended by ChatGPT or similar tools ahead of genuinely doing the underlying work. Be skeptical of anyone selling a shortcut here, since the tools themselves, and the companies building them, have not published any such mechanism, and the pattern across every AI system so far has been to reward real-world trust signals, not gamed ones.

The honest scale of this right now

For most small, local Philippine businesses, this channel is currently a smaller share of how customers find them than Google search itself, though it is growing. It is worth doing right because the underlying work overlaps almost entirely with what already matters for regular search visibility. It is not worth treating as an urgent separate project on its own, and it is not worth paying for a service that claims to specifically "optimize" for it apart from genuine SEO fundamentals.

Where this fits for a business with staff and systems, not just a solo operator

If you are past the solo-operator stage and running a business with staff, the practical takeaway is less about any single tactic and more about consistency across everything your business puts online, since that is what both Google and AI systems are reading as a trust signal. That kind of consistency across multiple channels, multiple staff members handling different things, and multiple review and citation touchpoints is exactly the kind of gap a proper audit surfaces, because it is hard to see clearly from inside your own business.
